# C. Sources: read depth and version hazards

This appendix records what a reference list cannot show: how deeply each source was read, and which entries
carry identifier hazards.

**Every source cited in this paper has been read in full**, which was not true of earlier drafts. Seven
entries postdated our literature review — three added after a citation audit, four surfaced by a simulated
review panel (§3.5) — and for a period all seven were cited at abstract, or title-and-author, depth. All
seven have since been read end to end, and doing so changed this paper in six places:
[munirathinam-2026-recuse] narrowed §5.1's claim and **removed** §5.5's novelty step; [cai-2025-tone],
which a draft called "squarely the paradigm §3 audits", is the opposite and is now §3.4's contrast case
(§7.3); [zhang-2026-politejudge] supplied §3.4's admitted confound and §8.4's numbers, and a draft called
its paraphrases "independently written", which it never says; [sun-2026-esteer]'s replan-frequency result
made "praise shortens" no longer unanticipated (§4.4); [benzion-2026]'s prime lands *between two complete
task episodes*, correcting §1.2; and [zhu-2026-spreadsheetbench2] expanded §8.2 while closing a question
§2.8 depends on.

Two prohibitions follow, stated so they are not quietly relaxed: **no magnitude may be imported from
[sun-2026-esteer]**, which reports no sample sizes, intervals or significance tests anywhere, and **nothing
may be attributed to [zhang-2026-termsbench] beyond its instrumentation**, which §8.5 names and we have not
run. Two entries were corrected on re-verification: [zhang-2026-politejudge] is accepted at RecSys '26, not
an unrefereed preprint, and [zhu-2026-spreadsheetbench2] has **fourteen** authors, not seven.

**Version and identifier hazards.** Four entries resolve to the wrong thing if cited casually — a higher
rate than we expected, and the argument for pinning versions rather than years. [munirathinam-2026-recuse] must be **cited at v4**: its v1 title names access-deny signals and contains no
mid-flight study, yet indexers still serve that title under the v4 DOI. [greshake-2023] must be cited
by **ACM DOI or pinned at arXiv v2**, the v1 title being "More than you've asked for", which does not
contain the phrase "indirect prompt injection".
[weinberger-hozez-2026] must be **cited at v6 (10 Sep 2026)**, which has six versions behind it, roughly 8×
growth since v1, and 4,644 valid runs. And [sun-2026-esteer]'s `2604` identifier implies
an April 2026 submission where arXiv and OpenAlex both give 9 March for v1; we record that and assert no
explanation.
